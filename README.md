# Climbing_Canvas
ClimbingCanvas is a specialized Android application designed for climbers to track and manage bouldering routes (boulders) on "spray walls." It allows users to take or upload photos of their climbing walls and digitally mark routes with precise, interactive hold indicators.
•
Gym & Wall Management: Organize your climbing data by gyms and individual spray walls.
•
Interactive Route Setting:
◦
Tap to place holds directly on the wall photo.
◦
Drag & Drop holds to fine-tune their position.
◦
Resize holds using intuitive +/- controls to match real-world hold sizes.
◦
Categorize holds by type: Start, Hand, Foot, or Top using a standardized color scheme.
•
Advanced Viewing Experience:
◦
Focus Mode: The background is automatically dimmed when viewing routes, making the markings stand out.
◦
Zoom & Pan: Fully interactive canvas for detailed inspection of routes.
◦
Color Legend: Integrated guide for hold types.
•
Persistent Data: Uses Room Database for local storage, ensuring all your routes and high-resolution wall photos remain accessible.
